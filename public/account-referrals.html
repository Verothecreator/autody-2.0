How your credit works

Made for using Autody.

  1. Share your personal link with someone new to Autody.
  2. They sign up through your link and verify their email.
  3. You each receive $10 in platform-only credit for eligible purchases and trading.

Referral credit, the assets it funds, and the proceeds from those assets cannot be withdrawn or sent to another account or wallet. Completing verification or waiting 90 days does not remove this restriction.

Referral credit is used first for USD purchases. If you combine it with your own funds, the reward-funded share stays restricted through buys, sales, and swaps. Your own eligible funds remain subject to our usual withdrawal rules.

One welcome reward per person. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, false information, and attempts to move rewards out of Autody may result in reversed rewards and bans for every account involved.
